# Changelog

Rule changes, newest first. The current method is in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md).

## 13 November 2026

- Ciudadanos's record no longer counts for Democracia 21. It is shown as "Pista". An external review pointed out that another party's old positions do not establish this party's.
- Results list shows how many answers were compared for parties with little data. Winner card says "Resultado provisional" until all statements are answered.
- "Pistas" started to count: partner and predecessor records, and approximate evidence with a clear direction.
- From 20 to 30 statements. Ten reserve statements added. "Spanish in all schools" retired (9 of 14 parties had no position of their own) and replaced by "co-official languages in Congress and the State administration".
- Star added: a marked statement counts double.
- Every party gets a percent from 2 comparable answers (before: 3, and at least a quarter of the voter's answers).
- Evidence widened step by step: public quotes, then votes, then approximate evidence. At first only programmes and official websites were allowed.
- Province lists checked against the BOE list of proclaimed candidacies.
- "A medias" scoring changed to the distance formula (75 % for a mild answer, 50 % for a strong one).

## 12 November 2026

- First version: 20 statements, positions from programmes only.

Raw research files, prompts and planning notes were removed from the repository on 13 November 2026 to keep it lean. They remain in the git history.
